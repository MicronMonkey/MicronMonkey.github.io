PART 01 · Q01–Q06

工會、會員與會費Unions, Membership and Dues

6 題
01一間公司真的可以有兩個工會嗎？合法嗎？Can one company have two unions? Is that legal?＋

不同組織範圍的企業工會，依法有可能並存。依《工會法》Labor Union Act第 6、9 條，企業工會可依廠場、事業單位或具有控制與從屬關係的企業組織；同一法定組織範圍原則上以一個企業工會為限。

美光關係企業工會並不是原台中工會的「上級工會」。是否符合各自的組織範圍，仍以章程、法人關係及主管機關登記認定為準。

Enterprise unions with different legally defined organizational scopes may coexist. Under Articles 6 and 9 of Taiwan's Labor Union Act, enterprise unions can be organized at the plant, business-entity, or affiliated-enterprise level. Only one enterprise union is permitted within the same organizational scope.

The Micron Affiliated Enterprises Union is not a superior union to the existing Taichung union. Actual eligibility depends on the bylaws, corporate relationships, and competent authority's registration assessment.

02加入美光關工，需要退出原本台中工會嗎？Do I need to leave the Taichung union to join the affiliated union?＋

不需要先退出台中工會。目前兩條路線同步推進，建議保留原工會會員身分，同時依資格申請加入美光關工。

能否同時具備會員身分，仍須符合兩個工會的章程及法定會員資格；加入新工會不會自動終止原工會會員資格。

No. You do not need to resign from the Taichung union first. The current dual-track approach recommends retaining existing union membership while voluntarily applying to the affiliated union.

Dual membership remains subject to both unions' bylaws and legal eligibility requirements. Joining another union does not automatically terminate existing membership.

03已經有台中工會，為什麼還需要美光關工？Why establish another union if Taichung already has one?＋

因為目前採取的是「雙線並進」，並不是二選一。

  • 原工會戰線：推動召開會員大會，要求會員參與決策、檢視 10/21 協議程序及效力。
  • 新工會戰線：跨廠區串聯員工，建立另一個團體協商與依法處理勞資爭議的管道。

共同目標是爭取公開、透明、合理的員工分潤制度與勞動尊嚴。

The strategy is two parallel tracks, not an either-or choice.

  • Existing union: Seek a general membership meeting, strengthen members' decision-making rights, and review the October 21 agreement and its procedures.
  • Affiliated union: Connect employees across Micron Taiwan sites and develop an additional channel for collective bargaining and lawful labor-dispute actions.

Both seek fair, transparent profit sharing and respect for workers.

04美光關工每月會費多少？有沒有入會費？How much are the affiliated union's dues and enrollment fee?＋

依目前公開的入會申請網頁，每月經常會費為新臺幣 50 元；另外，入會費標示為「依工會規定及通知繳納」，並未公開固定金額。

薪資代扣及經常會費、團結基金等月費的繳納方式，依工會相關決議辦理。是否還有其他費用，應以工會正式章程與公告為準。

法律提醒：《工會法》第 28 條對入會費及經常會費設有最低標準；公開申請頁列示的 50 元如何符合相關規定，仍有待工會進一步說明。

The current membership application page states NT$50 in regular monthly dues. It says the separate enrollment fee is payable as determined and notified by the union; no fixed enrollment amount is published.

Payroll deduction, regular dues, any solidarity fund contributions, and payment arrangements are subject to union decisions. Check official bylaws and announcements for all applicable fees.

Legal note: Article 28 of the Labor Union Act sets statutory minimums for enrollment and regular dues. The union should clarify how the advertised NT$50 arrangement complies with applicable rules.

05原本台中工會每月 250 元會費，可以取消嗎？Can I stop paying the current Taichung union's NT原本台中工會每月 250 元會費，可以取消嗎？50 monthly dues?＋

加入美光關工，不會自動取消台中工會每月 250 元的會費。若仍保有原工會會員資格，會費義務應依原工會章程及相關法規辦理。

停止扣繳、改變繳納方式或退會，都應先確認原工會章程與原本簽署的扣款授權。不要把「加入新工會」和「自動停扣原工會會費」視為同一件事。

Joining the affiliated union does not automatically cancel the existing NT$250 monthly union dues. If you remain a member, your payment obligations are determined by the original union's bylaws and applicable law.

Before stopping payroll deductions, changing payment methods, or resigning, check the existing union's bylaws and your deduction authorization. Joining a second union is not itself an instruction to stop paying the first.

06哪些美光員工可以申請加入？Which Micron employees can apply to join?＋

依公開入會申請頁，招募對象包括年滿 16 歲、符合章程資格的以下在台美光相關企業員工：

  • 台灣美光晶圓科技股份有限公司
  • 台灣美光記憶體股份有限公司
  • 美商美光亞太科技股份有限公司
  • 其他符合章程與法定組織範圍的美光在台關係企業

代表雇主行使管理權或人事管理職務者，可能有資格限制。填表不等於立即取得會員資格，須依入會程序繳費並經理事會審查通過。

According to the public application page, applicants aged 16 or older who meet the union bylaws may include employees of:

  • Micron Memory Taiwan Co., Ltd.
  • Micron Technology Taiwan Co., Ltd.
  • Micron's Asia-Pacific entity in Taiwan
  • Other affiliated Micron companies in Taiwan that fall within the legal and bylaw-defined scope

Certain personnel exercising employer or human-resources management authority may face restrictions. Submitting a form does not automatically confer membership: applicable fees and board approval are part of the admission process.

PART 02 · Q07–Q09

協商、罷工與協議效力Bargaining, Strikes and Agreement Validity

3 題
07美光關工可以獨立向公司提出協商嗎？Can the affiliated union bargain directly with the company?＋

符合資格的企業工會，依法可以提出團體協約協商。《團體協約法》Collective Agreement Act第 6 條規定，勞資雙方不得無正當理由拒絕協商。

不過，當兩個以上工會向相同雇主提出團體協約協商時，可能涉及共同推選協商代表；無法推選時，依會員人數比例分配。因此，有協商資格，不等於必然能排除其他工會、獨自主導協商。

A legally qualified enterprise union may request collective-agreement negotiations. Article 6 of the Collective Agreement Act prohibits parties from refusing negotiations without just cause.

If multiple unions seek negotiations with the same employer, rules on jointly selecting or allocating bargaining representatives may apply. Having bargaining rights does not guarantee the new union can unilaterally exclude another union.

08美光關工可以發動罷工嗎？需要台中工會同意嗎？Can the affiliated union strike without approval from the Taichung union?＋

原台中工會的同意，並非新工會依法行使爭議權的當然前提；但新工會成立或加入會員後，並不能直接發動罷工。

依法須先確認爭議事項是否可進行罷工，並完成適用的勞資爭議調解程序；依《勞資爭議處理法》Act for Settlement of Labor-Management Disputes第 54 條，工會也須經會員直接、無記名投票，取得全體會員過半數同意等法定條件。

具體爭議相對人、投票會員範圍及跨法人參與資格，需按實際案件認定。

Approval from the existing Taichung union is not automatically required for a separate union's lawful dispute actions. However, forming or joining the new union does not authorize an immediate strike.

The dispute must be legally eligible for a strike; applicable mediation procedures must be completed; and Article 54 of the Act for Settlement of Labor-Management Disputes requires a direct, secret-ballot vote approved by more than half of all relevant union members, among other requirements.

The specific employer, eligible voters, and multi-entity participation must be assessed for the particular dispute.

0910/21 簽署的協議，可以撤銷或重新協商嗎？Can the October 21 agreement be revoked or renegotiated?＋

加入新工會不會讓 10/21 協議自動失效。協議究竟屬於何種法律文件、是否經過適當授權與決議，以及能否撤銷或重新協商，都必須依協議全文、工會章程和相關法規判斷。

若屬《團體協約法》上的團體協約，第 9 條有簽訂決議及補行追認的規定；會員大會、會員代表大會的權限也應依法及章程判斷，不能一概視為僅有其中一種會議才有權決定。

Joining another union does not automatically invalidate the October 21 agreement. The agreement's legal status, authorization, decision-making procedures, and possible remedies depend on its actual wording, the union bylaws, and applicable laws.

If it is a collective agreement, Article 9 of the Collective Agreement Act addresses approval and subsequent ratification. The respective roles of a members' meeting and a delegates' meeting must be assessed under the law and bylaws rather than assuming one is always exclusively competent.

連署資料包含個人資料，應限於必要目的交付、控管閱覽權限並妥善保存；並非只因屬工會連署，就能保證完全不會外洩。

The petition has exceeded the currently calculated statutory signature threshold and is being prepared for formal submission.

A planned in-person handover to the Taichung union chair, Mark Hsu, could not take place due to a schedule change. The current plan is to submit petition materials to the Central Taiwan Science Park Bureau on Tuesday, November 3, while coordinating a renewed handover and possible on-site receipt or witnessing with the chair.

These are plans, not confirmation that the chair will attend or that official service has already occurred. Actual delivery, the legally appropriate receiving authority, and proof of receipt will be reported after completion.

Petition records contain personal information. They must be transferred only as necessary, with access controls and appropriate safeguards. Union-petition status alone does not guarantee that data cannot leak.

11參與連署或加入新工會，工作權益會受影響嗎？Could signing the petition or joining another union affect my job?＋

《工會法》第 35 條禁止雇主因勞工組織、加入或參加工會活動等法定事由而給予不利待遇。依法參與工會活動具有法律保障。

不過，法律保護不等於姓名、工號或申請資料絕對不會被看見。建議確認資料用途、保存及存取政策；如遭遇疑似不當施壓或不利待遇，請保留紀錄並尋求工會或法律專業協助。

Article 35 of the Labor Union Act prohibits employers from treating workers adversely for legally protected union organization, membership, or activities. Lawful union participation is legally protected.

However, this does not guarantee complete confidentiality of names, employee IDs, or application details. Review data-use and retention policies, preserve evidence of any suspected retaliation, and seek union or legal assistance when appropriate.
