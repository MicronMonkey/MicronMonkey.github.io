緊急撤銷簽約授權、暫緩簽署暨請求召開臨時會員大會連署書

Petition to Review Signing Authorization, Suspend Execution and Convene an Extraordinary Union Meeting
就 2026 年 10 月 21 日勞資協議／團體協約相關案
Concerning the labor-management agreement / collective agreement matter of October 21, 2026
受文者 / To: 台灣美光記憶體企業工會理事會及談判代表
Board of Directors and Negotiation Representatives, Taiwan Micron Memory Enterprise Union
副知 / Cc: 台灣美光記憶體股份有限公司
Micron Technology Taiwan (company name to be legally verified)
性質 / Type: 工會會員個人具名意見及請求
Individual member statement and petition
版本 / Version: 2026-10-23 / V11 (ZH–EN)

主旨 / Subject: 本人就 2026 年 10 月 21 日相關勞資協議／團體協約草案之內容、簽署授權及審議程序提出異議，請求停止依原授權逕行簽署、暫緩簽署、公開最終全文，並依法及依工會章程重新審議，以維護會員參與及知悉權益。I express objections concerning the draft labor-management / collective agreement addressed on October 21, 2026, including its terms, signing authorization, and review procedures. I request that execution under the disputed prior authorization be suspended, the final text be disclosed in full, and the matter be reconsidered in accordance with applicable law and the union constitution.

一、連署聲明及具體請求 / I. Statement and Requests
  1. （一）表明撤銷原支持意願並請求重新確認授權：本人不再支持就本案先前所為簽署授權，請工會依法及依章程檢視授權決議、範圍與效力，停止逕依爭議授權簽署。Withdrawal of prior support and review of authorization: I no longer support the previously granted signing authorization for this matter. I request that the union review its resolution, scope, and effect under the law and its constitution, and refrain from signing solely on the basis of the disputed authorization.
  2. （二）要求暫緩簽署及全文公開：在協議或團體協約最終版本完整公開、會員得充分知悉內容並完成必要審議程序前，請工會及談判代表暫緩簽署。Suspension and full disclosure: I request that the union and its representatives refrain from executing the agreement until the final text has been fully disclosed, members have had a reasonable opportunity to review it, and all required procedures are completed.
  3. （三）請求召開臨時會員大會、追認及檢討理監事責任：依下方具名正式請求，請求理事長召開會員大會臨時會議，審議勞資協議之追認程序、是否依工會章程第40條事先取得會員大會授權，及解任、改選本屆理、監事之必要性。Request an extraordinary general meeting, review of ratification and board accountability: Convene a special general meeting to consider ratification, whether prior authorization from the general meeting was obtained under Article 40 of the union bylaws, and whether the current directors and supervisors should be removed and re-elected. See the formal signed request below.
  4. （四）通知相關當事人並保留權利：請將此具名意見列入正式紀錄並通知資方；本人保留向工會內部、勞工主管機關或司法機關依法提出異議及救濟之權利。Record, notice, and reservation of rights: Please record this statement and notify the employer. I reserve any lawful right to raise objections or seek remedies through union procedures, labor authorities, or the courts.
二、召開臨時會員大會之正式請求 / II. Formal Request for Extraordinary General Meeting

主旨：請求召集工會會員大會臨時會議。

內容：本人為台灣美光記憶體股份有限公司企業工會會員（員工證如附件），因認本工會與台灣美光記憶體股份有限公司間於民國115年10月21日所私自簽訂之勞資協議（團體協約），有依團體協約法第9條第2項規定辦理追認程序以定其效力之必要；另就工會理事長未依工會章程第40條規定，事先取得會員大會授權，即擅自與公司簽署前開勞資協議乙情，亦認有解任及改選本屆理、監事之必要，爰依工會法第23條第3項規定，請求理事長召集工會會員大會臨時會議。

English reference translation (not part of the lawyer-reviewed Chinese text): I am a member of the enterprise labor union of Micron Technology Taiwan (employee badge copy attached). I believe that the labor-management agreement (collective agreement) entered into privately between the union and Micron Technology Taiwan on October 21, 2026 requires a ratification procedure under Article 9, Paragraph 2 of the Collective Agreement Act to determine its effect. Further, because the union chairperson signed the aforementioned agreement with the company without first obtaining authorization from the general meeting of union members as required under Article 40 of the union bylaws, I also consider it necessary to remove and re-elect the incumbent directors and supervisors. Accordingly, pursuant to Article 23, Paragraph 3 of the Labor Union Act, I request that the chairperson convene an extraordinary general meeting of union members.
