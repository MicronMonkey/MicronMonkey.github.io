一、連署聲明及具體請求 / I. Statement and Requests
  1. （一）表明撤銷原支持意願並請求重新確認授權：本人不再支持就本案先前所為之簽署授權，請工會依法及依章程檢視原授權決議、程序、範圍與效力，並停止將有爭議之授權作為未經必要會員審議之後續處理依據。1.1 Withdrawal of prior support and review of authorization: I withdraw my prior support for the signing authorization in this matter.   2. （二）要求公開全文及重新審議：請工會公開 2026 年 10 月 21 日協議之最終全文、相關授權決議及審議紀錄，提供會員合理查閱機會，並依法及依章程檢視是否須辦理追認或重新審議程序。1.2 Full disclosure and review: I request that the union disclose the full final text of the October 21, 2026 agreement and relevant authorization and deliberation records, allow members a reasonable opportunity to review them, and examine whether ratification or renewed deliberation is required under applicable laws and union bylaws.
